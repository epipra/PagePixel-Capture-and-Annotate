Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Summary

PagePixel — Capture & Annotate does not collect or sell your browsing data or personal information, and has no analytics or tracking. Screenshots stay on your device unless you click Upload. Only then is that one image sent to PagePixel's image host, where it becomes publicly viewable by anyone who has its link.

What PagePixel does

PagePixel captures screenshots of the page you're currently viewing (visible area, full page, or a selected region), lets you annotate them in an editor, and exports the result as an image or PDF — downloaded to your computer, copied to your clipboard, or, if you choose, uploaded to get a shareable link.

Uploading and sharing

  • Only when you click Upload. Nothing is uploaded automatically, in the background, or for any capture you don't explicitly upload.
  • What is sent: the current image, including your annotations, in the format you selected (PNG, JPEG, WEBP, or PDF). The page's address, its title, and any other page content are not sent, and there is no account or sign-in.
  • Where it goes: to PagePixel's upload service at upload.omwly.com, which stores the file at img.omwly.com. Both run on Cloudflare (Cloudflare Workers and R2 storage).
  • Who can see it: anyone who has the link. Each link contains a long random code, so it can't be guessed or found by browsing, but it is public: anyone you share it with, or anyone they pass it on to, can open it without signing in.
  • How long it's kept: uploaded images are stored permanently. Don't upload anything you wouldn't want to be public — use the Blur or Pixelate tools to hide sensitive details first.
  • Your IP address is used only momentarily to limit how many uploads can be made per minute, to prevent abuse. PagePixel does not store it or any other information about you. As the hosting provider, Cloudflare processes network requests under its own privacy policy.
  • Removal: to have an uploaded image deleted, contact us through the PagePixel Chrome Web Store listing and include its link.

What data we access, and why

  • activeTab — lets PagePixel capture and, when you use Full Page or Selected Area mode, briefly scroll the page you click the toolbar icon on. PagePixel only acts on the tab you explicitly invoke it from.
  • scripting — used to inject the small on-page helper script that measures page dimensions, scrolls the page for Full Page capture, and draws the drag-to-select overlay for Selected Area capture. It runs only when you trigger a capture.
  • downloads — used only when you click Download, to save your exported image/PDF to your computer.
  • clipboardWrite — used only when you click Copy, to place the image on your system clipboard.
  • storage — remembers your last-used capture mode, delay, and direction settings locally, so the popup opens with your preferences next time.

A captured image is also held briefly in your browser's local IndexedDB (no special permission required for this) while it's handed off from the capture step to the editor tab, and is deleted as soon as the editor loads it.

Uploading needs no extra permission: it is a normal web request from the extension to upload.omwly.com, made only when you click Upload.

PagePixel does not request access to your browsing history, does not run on pages you haven't explicitly captured from, and does not include any remote code, analytics, or third-party trackers.

Data sharing

The only data that ever leaves your browser is an image you choose to upload, which is stored and served publicly as described above. PagePixel does not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties for advertising or any other purpose, and does not use uploaded images for anything other than hosting them at their link.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change and the update will ship with a new version of the extension.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or requests to remove an uploaded image, can be sent through PagePixel's Chrome Web Store listing (the feedback ✉ button on the result screen opens it).
